Privacy Policy

Pathways · Last updated [date]

The short version. You can play Pathways without an account, and then everything stays on your device: we never see it. If you make an account for cloud saves, we keep your email address and a copy of your saves, only so your lives are on every device you sign in on. No ads, no tracking, no selling your data. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from inside the game.

Who we are

Pathways is made by [your name or company]. For anything about your data, write to [contact email].

Playing without an account

Your lives, settings and anything you type into the game (including names you add under People you know) are saved in your browser's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's data for the game, or deleting the app, removes them. Settings → Transfer saves makes a file you move yourself; we never see it.

If you make an account (cloud saves)

We keep:

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in, and to send a password reset when you ask for one.
Your passwordTo sign you in. It is stored scrambled (hashed) by our database provider; nobody can read it, including us.
A copy of your savesTo put the same lives on every device you sign in on. This includes anything you typed into the game, such as names you added under People you know.
The kind of device that last saved (for example "iPad" or "Windows"), and whenTo combine saves correctly when two devices save at once.

We use these only to run cloud saves. We do not use them for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell or share them, except with the services below that run the game for us.

Services that run the game

  • Supabase stores accounts and cloud saves. Supabase's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages serves the game's files. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when your device downloads them. GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides some of the game's fonts. Your device downloads them from Google, which sees your IP address. Google Fonts and privacy.

These services may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States.

No ads, no analytics, no cookies for tracking

Pathways has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It uses your browser's storage only to keep your saves and to keep you signed in.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete it, your email address, password and cloud saves are deleted straight away. A copy may stay in our provider's backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Cloud saves → Delete my account removes your email address, password and cloud saves. The lives on your device stay.
  • Delete only your cloud saves: Settings → Cloud saves → Delete my saves from the cloud.
  • Get a copy: Settings → Transfer saves gives you everything in a file.
  • Correct or ask about your data: write to [contact email].

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you have the right to access, correct, delete and take a copy of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Children

Pathways is not meant for children under 13, and children under 13 should not make an account. If you believe a child under 13 has made one, write to [contact email] and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to the game and to cloud saves are encrypted (HTTPS). Each player's saves can be read and changed only by that player's signed-in account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change matters to how your data is used, the game will tell you before it takes effect.
