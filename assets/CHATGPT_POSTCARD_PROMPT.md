# Honeymoon postcards: prompts for ChatGPT

One picture per country in the game (108), plus four general cards. The game puts the words on top ("Greetings from …"
in the couple's color, the stamp and the postmark), so **the pictures must have no text in them at all**.

## How to make them

1. Paste the **style prompt** below into ChatGPT, then add the country's scene after `Scene:`.
2. Make them all in one long chat if you can, so they come out as one matching set. If one drifts, say "same style as
   the others, softer colors".
3. **Landscape, 3:2** (1536 × 1024 is ideal). PNG or JPG is fine.
4. **Name each file by its code**, e.g. `fr.png` for France, `pf.png` for French Polynesia, then upload them in the
   chat. I'll shrink them, put them in `assets/postcards/` and switch each country over. Any country without a picture
   keeps the drawn card it has now.

## Style prompt (the same every time)

> A cute, cozy cartoon illustration for a vintage travel postcard, in a soft storybook style: rounded shapes, gentle
> shading and warm light, rich but soft colors, a little painterly texture, charming and romantic, like a
> honeymoon destination. Wide landscape composition. Keep the top-left third of the sky simple and uncluttered (a
> title will be written there). No text, no letters, no signs with words, no people, no borders, no frame.
> Scene: [SCENE]
