## Avatar art

All avatar art in `assets/` (heads, faces, eyes, eyebrows, lips, hair, clothes, jewelry, crowns, aging and emotions) was
made for From Birth:

- Base heads, templates and the fitting tools (`scripts/`) were drawn and written for this project.
- Hair, clothing and some accessory shapes were generated with ChatGPT (OpenAI) from the prompts in
  `assets/CHATGPT_*_PROMPT.md`, then traced, cleaned and fitted to every head with the scripts in `scripts/`.
  OpenAI's terms assign the output to the user; Andre Subero uses it under those terms.
- No art was taken from stock sites, asset packs or other games.

## Data and sources

Real-world facts the game draws on (laws, history, tax rules, health guidance) are cited where they are used in the
code: for example `js/data/inheritance.js` (PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Wikipedia), `js/data/health/contraception.js`
(CDC, ACOG, NHS, FDA) and `js/data/investigations.js` (the agencies' own career pages). All people, companies,
universities, royal families, brands and leagues in the game are invented.

## Code

From Birth uses no third-party code libraries: everything in `js/` was written for the game.
